Student pilot to private pilot

Your path to the left seat

The steps every private pilot goes through, in order.

  1. Discovery flightTake the controls with an instructor and see if flying is for you.
  2. Medical & student certificateGet your FAA medical and apply for your student pilot certificate through IACRA.
  3. First soloAfter mastering takeoffs, landings and emergencies, fly the pattern alone.
  4. Cross-countryPlan and fly to other airports, including your solo cross-countries.
  5. Knowledge testPass the FAA written exam after ground school.
  6. CheckrideFly with an FAA examiner and earn your private pilot certificate.
